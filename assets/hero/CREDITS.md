from that project's own website or GitHub repository. No stock imagery, no
placeholders, no images from unrelated sites. Each image was cropped to 16:9
and resized to 1920x1080 for use as a FIN-DSP hero banner; the software shown
is unchanged.

## Notes

- No substitutions were needed: all six target projects had a usable screenshot
  on their own site or repo.
- hero-05-audacity.jpg was cropped right-aligned rather than centre-cropped,
  so the Audacity window stays fully in frame and the empty black area on the
  left of the source image is dropped. All other images are centre-cropped.
- Screenshots are credited to their projects; the software licenses above are
  the licenses of the pictured applications, not of these cropped images.
  Images are used here as the projects themselves publish them, with a link
  back to the project's own site/repo as the source.
