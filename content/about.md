          <div class="module-body">
            <h2 style="font-family:var(--heading-font, &quot;Rajdhani&quot;, sans-serif); font-size:20px; margin:0 0 8px;">FINDSP — Audio Research &amp; Music Technology Division</h2>
            <p>FINDSP is the audio research and music-technology division of CTP-EOS Linux, operating within the broader CTP / CTP Energy ecosystem.</p>
            <p>We design and develop advanced audio software technologies for modern production environments, supporting most major DAWs through VST2 / VST3 formats. Our work emphasizes vanguard, experimental, and forward-looking VST / VSTi concepts rather than conventional commodity plugins.</p>
            <h3 style="font-size:16px; margin:14px 0 6px;">Software Audio &amp; Music-Tech Focus</h3>
            <p>FINDSP's project line is centered on future-centric, high-end audio systems, including:</p>
            <ul>
              <li>Advanced software instruments and synthesizer architectures</li>
              <li>Experimental effects and signal-processing concepts</li>
              <li>Novel signal flow, modulation, and control paradigms</li>
            </ul>
            <p>Deep integration with Linux-based creative workflows via CTP-EOS.</p>
            <p>Our goal is to explore what comes next in music technology — beyond traditional plugin design — bridging audio engineering, system-level integration, and emerging research concepts.</p>
            <h3 style="font-size:16px; margin:14px 0 6px;">Potential Hardware Exploration</h3>
            <p>Subject to market conditions, pricing strategy, and product-market fit, FINDSP may explore branded hardware concepts in the future, including synthesizers and hybrid software-hardware systems. Any such efforts would remain aligned with our research-driven, experimental philosophy.</p>
            <h3 style="font-size:16px; margin:14px 0 6px;">About the Founder — Resofactor</h3>
            <p>Resofactor is the founder of FINDSP and the architect behind CTP-EOS Linux and CTP Energy Science (CTPSci) — a developing framework focused on next-generation systems, non-conventional computation, and advanced signal models.</p>
            <p>Before dedicating his work to CTP-focused research, Reso has long been immersed in electronic and experimental music culture, with influences spanning:</p>
            <p>Psytrance, IDM, EDM, Industrial, EBM, Ambient, Dark Ambient, Psybient, Chill, Techno, Lounge, and other electronic sub-genres.</p>
            <p>That background continues to inform FINDSP's design philosophy: tools built by and for explorers, not just producers.</p>
