About FIN-DSP

FINDSP is the audio research and music-technology division of CTP-EOS Linux, operating within the broader CTP / CTP Energy ecosystem.

We design and develop advanced audio software technologies for modern production environments, supporting most major DAWs through VST2 / VST3 formats. Our work emphasizes vanguard, experimental, and forward-looking VST / VSTi concepts rather than conventional commodity plugins.

Submit a Project

Got an audio project? Get it listed.

FIN-DSP lists new audio software projects as they appear — plugins, DSP, synthesizers, effects, audio engines, DAWs, libraries, frameworks, and tools.

To get your project listed, open an issue on the FIN-DSP org on GitHub with your repository link. The automated discovery engine is in development and will find new audio projects on GitHub and list them here.

There is no submission form yet — contact runs through the org.
