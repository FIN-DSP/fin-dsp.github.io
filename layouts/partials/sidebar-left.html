About FIN-DSP

FIN-DSP is a live project news and discovery portal for the entire audio software development community.

Tens of thousands of audio developers publish projects on GitHub — finding them takes hours of digging. FIN-DSP discovers new audio software projects as they appear and publishes them here, with links back to the original developer and repository.

Open-source audio DSP, curated from GitHub, SourceForge, and the abandonware shelf. Commercial releases to come.

About FIN-DSP

FINDSP is the audio research and music-technology division of CTP-EOS Linux, operating within the broader CTP / CTP Energy ecosystem.

We design and develop advanced audio software technologies for modern production environments, supporting most major DAWs through VST2 / VST3 formats. Our work emphasizes vanguard, experimental, and forward-looking VST / VSTi concepts rather than conventional commodity plugins.
